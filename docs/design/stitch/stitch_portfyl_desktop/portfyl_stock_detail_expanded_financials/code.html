About NVIDIA Corporation

NVIDIA Corporation is a global leader in accelerated computing. The company's pioneer work in GPU computing has transformed the gaming, professional visualization, data center, and automotive markets. With the rise of artificial intelligence, NVIDIA's technologies have become the bedrock of the modern data center, powering the world's most sophisticated AI models.

Founded in 1993, the company is headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Its primary revenue streams are derived from its Compute & Networking segment, which includes Data Center platforms and systems for AI, and its Graphics segment, featuring the GeForce GPUs for gaming and the NVIDIA RTX for professional design work.

Currently, the company is experiencing unprecedented growth driven by the "AI Industrial Revolution." Its H100 and upcoming Blackwell architecture represent the pinnacle of semiconductor engineering, allowing for massive-scale large language model training and inference that defines the current technological landscape.
